STUDY S08 · IMBALANCED TABULAR CLASSIFICATION

S08 — Threshold Correction, Not Rebalancing: A Pre-registered Generalization Study of Benchmark-Practice Inflation in Imbalanced Tabular Classification

A confirmatory study separating the effect of correcting the default decision threshold from the additional effect of imbalance treatments. It compares conventional and prevalence-corrected decisions, then evaluates class weighting, SMOTE, and random undersampling against the corrected control using HistGradientBoosting and Logistic Regression. The final results include 85 datasets; the SMOTE contrasts use 77.

STUDY S07 · BENCHMARK-PRACTICE INFLATION

S07 — Does Class-Imbalance Correction Beat a Threshold-Corrected Control? A Catalog-Wide Confirmatory Study of Benchmark-Practice Bias in Imbalanced Classification

A catalog-wide confirmatory comparison of a conventional classifier threshold, a threshold-corrected control, and three imbalance-handling methods: SMOTE, cost-sensitive Random Forest, and EasyEnsemble. It tests whether the apparent benefit attributed to imbalance correction remains after the baseline threshold is corrected. The analysed cohort contains 98 datasets from 131 candidates.

STUDY S06 · ARBITER REVISION SUITE

S06 — Arbiter Revision Suite: A Multi-Stage Evaluation of Utility-Signal Selection, Decision-Threshold Recalibration, and Candidate-Pool Expansion in the NeuroCortex Self-Reinforcing Development Framework

A multi-stage investigation of how the SRDF Arbiter's decisions change when its utility signal, decision threshold, and candidate pool are revised. The confirmatory v2 evaluation used a 198-dataset cohort and 600 triggered cases; later v3 and v4 revisions were adaptive/exploratory extensions. In the reported v4 comparison, the Arbiter remained 0.95 percentage points below standalone EasyEnsemble.

STUDY S05 · EXTENDED COMPARATIVE EVALUATION

S05 — Extended Comparative Evaluation of NeuroCortex SRDF Against SMOTE+Random Forest, Cost-Sensitive Random Forest, ADASYN+Random Forest, and EasyEnsemble on a Frozen OpenML Cohort

An extended comparison on the same frozen 45-dataset OpenML cohort used in S04. It evaluates SRDF v1 against SMOTE+Random Forest, cost-sensitive Random Forest, ADASYN+Random Forest, and EasyEnsemble across 1,125 planned fold-level cases, and examines counterfactual Arbiter decisions and negative-performance cases. SRDF v1 ranked fourth of five; the Arbiter policy was 1.93 percentage points below the always-SMOTE+RF policy on 373 successful cases.

STUDY S04 · OPENML GENERALIZATION EVALUATION

S04 — Empirical Generalization Evaluation of NeuroCortex SRDF on OpenML Binary Tabular Classification Datasets: A Frozen 45-Dataset Cohort with Repeated Stratified Cross-Validation

An empirical evaluation of the SRDF adaptation workflow on 45 frozen OpenML binary-classification datasets. Each dataset was evaluated using five random seeds and five stratified folds per seed, producing 1,125 fold-level cases. The run recorded 373 successful adaptations; a separate dataset-level analysis across 15 datasets reported a mean balanced-accuracy change of +0.028805.

STUDY S01 · FOUNDATIONAL PROTOTYPE VALIDATION

S01 — Foundational Executable Prototype of NeuroCortex SRDF: Feasibility Demonstration of an End-to-End, Evidence-Governed Runtime Structural Adaptation Workflow

The first executable demonstration of NeuroCortex SRDF. Using a bounded illustrative runtime setting, it exercises the full sequence: observe the context (including class-imbalance information), analyse it, generate a candidate structural adaptation, evaluate the candidate, authorize or reject it through the Arbiter, and record the outcome. Its purpose is to establish implementation feasibility of the complete workflow; it is not a benchmark and makes no claim of statistical significance or predictive superiority.
