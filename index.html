01 / ABOUT

Research built on evidence.

My research examines how experimental design, evaluation choices, and adaptive system architectures shape the reliability of machine-learning conclusions.

The work emphasizes pre-specified methods, traceable research records, reproducible execution, and clear separation between protocols, empirical results, software artifacts, and peer-reviewed publications.

STUDY S08 · IMBALANCED TABULAR CLASSIFICATION

Threshold Correction, Not Rebalancing: A Pre-registered Generalization Study of Benchmark-Practice Inflation in Imbalanced Tabular Classification

A pre-registered generalization study testing whether apparent balanced-accuracy gains attributed to rebalancing methods can instead arise from the use of an uncorrected default decision threshold. The study evaluates the finding across learners and datasets.
