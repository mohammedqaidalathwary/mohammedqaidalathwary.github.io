01 / ABOUT

Research built on evidence.

I work on questions at the intersection of machine learning evaluation and adaptive AI architecture. My research emphasizes transparent experimental design, reproducible evidence, and careful interpretation of model performance.

My current work examines how evaluation choices shape conclusions in imbalanced classification, alongside the design and empirical study of frameworks for controlled structural adaptation in AI systems.
