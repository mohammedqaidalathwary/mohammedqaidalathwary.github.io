## Research studies and Zenodo records

Study identifiers (S06, S07, S08) are internal project labels. Public-facing study headings describe the scientific subject; individual research stages are listed separately. Concept DOIs group all versions of a record, while Version DOIs identify a specific archived deposit.

### S07 — Benchmark-Practice Inflation in Imbalanced Classification

| Research stage | Record | Concept DOI | Version DOI |
|---|---|---|---|

## Record interpretation

- A **Concept DOI** identifies a work across its versions and resolves to the latest version.
- A **Version DOI** identifies one specific archived version.
- A Zenodo DOI establishes a persistent research record; it does not by itself indicate peer review or journal publication.
- Protocols, results datasets, software releases, and preprints are described according to their record type.
